Can drivers really see the stars?

The two emitters draw a red star on the road on each side of your rear wheel, so a driver sees a red zone on the asphalt, not only a point of light on your bike. How bright it looks depends on the road surface and on how dark it is around you: strongest on dark asphalt at night, faint under streetlights.

Does it fit my bike?

It straps around the seat post with a rubber band. Round or oval post, road, gravel or commuter bike: no tools, and it comes off as fast as it went on.

Is there a normal tail light too?

Yes. The vertical red lens is a rear light. The two laser stars are added underneath it.

How long does delivery take?

Use it right

  • Point the lasers at the road behind you, never at people, animals, vehicles or windows.
  • Do not look into the emitters.
  • It adds to a rear light; it does not replace reflectors or a front light.
  • Check the rules for bike lighting where you ride.

Laser tail light FAQ

What's in the box?

The StarLane light and its rubber strap. We list anything else (cable, manual) once we have opened the box ourselves.

Does it work in the rain?

We don't claim a water-resistance rating until we have tested it. Treat it like any small bike light for now: fine in a drizzle, take it off for a pressure wash.

Will the stars show on a wet or light-coloured road?

Red laser light shows best on dark, matte asphalt at night. On wet road the stars still show but reflect more; on light concrete or under bright streetlights they are fainter.

That is how most people found us. Two lights, one for each riding buddy, is the usual way to give it.
